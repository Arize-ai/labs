TITLE: A still pond reflecting a moon absent from the sky

IDEA: Dreaming is the mind showing itself something vivid that has no source in the world, as the water holds a bright moon the sky above does not contain.

COMPOSITION: A level horizon running across the card at y 118 from x 84 to x 316. Above it, open night sky. At (200, 80), where the moon should be, a ghost circle of radius 24 with nothing inside it. Below the horizon, the pond's surface fills y 118–194. On it, the reflected moon is a disc of radius 24 centred at (200, 156). It is sliced into five horizontal bands, each about 6 units tall, separated by thin gaps of dark water, so it reads as a reflection broken by slow ripples. A soft glow of radius about 70 spreads from the reflection across the water, wider than tall (roughly 140 wide by 60 tall), and stays below the horizon. Six or seven long, gently curving ripple strokes cross the water at y 130, 140, 150, 162, 172, 182. They are longest near the reflection, about 120 units, and shorter and fainter toward the edges.

LIGHT: The reflected moon bands are the brightest mass at tone 1.0, with dots kissing. Each band's top edge sits slightly brighter than its bottom, as if lit from the upper left. The glow on the water falls from 0.55 beside the disc to nothing by its rim, shrinking dot by dot. It is brightest in a vertical column directly below and above the reflection, like a moon-path. The water directly under the horizon has a faint band at 0.15, so the line has something to sit on. The sky stays at background texture throughout, including inside the ghost circle, which gets no light at all. Five or six isolated pinprick stars at tone 0.2–0.3 are scattered in the upper sky, none within 40 units of the ghost circle. The gaps between the moon bands and everything outside the glow stay dark.

LINES: The horizon is a full-strength solid line. The ghost moon in the sky is a faint dashed circle tracing its outline: the one clue to what is missing. The ripple strokes are half-strength solid lines near the reflection, fading to faint at their outer ends. The outline of the reflected disc is not drawn; the bands define it.

ORANGE: The reflected moon's bands are printed in orange. It is the one bright thing on the plate and the thing that isn't really there. The eye lands on it, then travels up to the empty dashed circle.

VARIATION: Vary the ripple distortion, so each band of the reflected moon shifts sideways by up to 4 units and differs slightly in width. Also vary the positions of the scattered pinprick stars.
