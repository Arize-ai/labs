TITLE: Floating lanterns drifting down a winding night river

IDEA: An event stream is a steady, ordered flow of discrete occurrences, and lanterns set adrift on a river are exactly that: separate lights carried one after another by a current toward the viewer.

COMPOSITION:
- **Horizon:** a horizon at y 70, spanning x 110–290.
- **River:** a river seen in perspective, an S-curve band whose centreline runs from (200, 70) at the horizon through (168, 104), (226, 146) and out the bottom at (196, 194). It is only 6 units wide at the horizon and widens smoothly to 76 units at the bottom edge, so it reads as coming toward us.
- **Lanterns:** nine lanterns sit on the centreline as small discs, spaced unevenly but in order. They grow from radius 2 at the far end to radius 10 at the near end:
  - (199, 74) r2
  - (190, 82) r2.5
  - (176, 94) r3
  - (170, 108) r3.5
  - (184, 122) r4.5
  - (208, 134) r5
  - (228, 148) r6
  - (220, 166) r8
  - (200, 184) r10
- **Reflections:** each lantern has a reflection, a short vertical streak directly below it on the water. Each streak is as long as 1.5× the lantern's radius and as wide as half its radius.

LIGHT:
- **Lanterns:** each lantern is full light (1.0), with a glow falling off over about 3× its radius. The glows of the near lanterns pool onto the water.
- **River:** the water is a faint mid-tone (0.25), slightly brighter (0.35) along the inner side of each bend and toward the near end. It fades to 0.15 at the far end.
- **Reflections:** the streaks are 0.5, broken up so they read as rippled.
- **What stays dark:** the banks on either side of the river and the sky above the horizon are left as bare background texture. There is no moon and no stars.

LINES:
- **Banks:** the two riverbanks are traced as half-strength solid lines that converge toward the horizon.
- **Horizon:** a faint solid line, broken where the river meets it.
- **Current:** three or four faint dashed lines run mid-river parallel to the banks, suggesting the current.
- **Lantern edges:** each lantern has a thin full-strength outline, so the sequence of lights survives at thumbnail size.

ORANGE: The lantern at (228, 148), its outline and its glow are orange. It is the one event in the stream the eye should catch, the one being looked at closely as it drifts by, mid-flow, with lights both before and after it.

VARIATION: Jitter the lanterns' positions along and across the current by a few units, and vary their spacing slightly so the rhythm of events differs on each print. Also randomise the ripple breaks in the reflections.
