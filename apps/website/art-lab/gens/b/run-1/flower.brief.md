TITLE: A single open cosmos bloom on a slender stem, botanical plate

IDEA: A flower is the moment a plant turns outward and offers itself to the light, so one bloom tilted up to the upper-left sun, its pollen-tipped heart glowing, carries the word plainly and quietly.

COMPOSITION: The flower head is seen in three-quarter view, tilted up and to the left. It is an elliptical disc of eight petals centred at (205, 100), spanning about 150 wide and 90 tall (the ellipse foreshortens the circle).
  - Each petal is a long rounded teardrop about 62 long and 24 wide at its widest. Its tip has three tiny notches. All eight radiate from the centre.
  - The two petals at the lower right sit slightly behind the disc and are partly hidden.
  - The central disc is an ellipse 26 wide and 18 tall at (205, 100).
  - A ring of about 14 short stamens, each 5–7 long, stands up around the disc rim.
  - The stem leaves the underside of the head at (212, 118). It curves gently down and slightly left to end at (188, 194), thin and faintly S-shaped.
  - One narrow lance-shaped leaf branches from the stem at (196, 160) and reaches up-right to (248, 142). It is about 10 wide at its middle.
  - One small closed bud on a short side stem sits at (150, 150), an oval 9 by 14 leaning left.

LIGHT:
  - **Upper-left petals:** These are the brightest mass, at tone 0.9 near their tips. Each fades toward the centre to about 0.55, so the petals read as a cupped bowl.
  - **Lower-right petals:** Tone 0.45–0.6. The two partly hidden ones sit at 0.35.
  - **Petal veins:** Each petal carries a faint inner falloff along its midline, a slightly darker ridge one dot wide, to suggest the veining.
  - **Central disc:** Tone 0.3, a darker cushion, with its upper-left edge at 0.45.
  - **Glow:** A soft glow of light (0.25 fading to 0) surrounds the whole head to a radius of about 95. It is strongest on the upper-left side.
  - **Stem:** Tone 0.4, brighter on its left edge.
  - **Leaf:** Tone 0.35, rising to 0.5 along its upper edge.
  - **Bud:** Tone 0.5.
  - **Dark areas:** Everything else stays background texture. This includes the space between stem and leaf, the area under the head, and all corners.

LINES:
  - Each petal's outline: full, solid.
  - Petal midribs: faint, solid, drawn from the base to about two-thirds of the length.
  - Central disc outline: half, solid.
  - Stem: drawn as a pair of edges, the left edge full and solid, the right edge half and solid.
  - Leaf outline: full, solid, with its midrib faint and solid.
  - Bud outline: half, solid, with its short stem drawn as a single half-strength solid line.
  - One faint dashed line: a very shallow arc from the bloom's centre toward the upper left, ending at (120, 60). It is the plate's only nod to "toward the light" and should be very understated.

ORANGE: The stamen tips, about 14 small pollen dots on the stamen ring, plus a tight cluster of orange dots filling the top half of the central disc. The eye should land on the flower's heart, the working part that the petals exist to display.

VARIATION:
  - The tilt and rotation of the whole head: rotate it ±8° and vary the foreshortening ratio slightly.
  - Each petal's length and width: about ±10% each, so the bloom looks grown rather than drawn by compass.
