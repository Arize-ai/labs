TITLE: A short pier pointing at one far light across dark water

IDEA: Longing is the pier that runs out toward a distant light and stops short, so all the reaching is visible and the arrival never comes.

COMPOSITION:
- Horizon: a level line across the card at y 110, from x 84 to x 316.
- Far light: a small bright point of radius 3 centred at (252, 106), sitting just above the horizon, wrapped in a glow of radius about 34.
- Reflection: a broken vertical column of short horizontal streaks below the light on the water, centred on x 252. It starts at y 113 and runs down to y 178. The streaks are about 14 wide near the horizon and widen to about 30 at the bottom.
- Pier deck: a trapezoid in perspective, aimed at the light but ending well before it.
  - Near end spans x 140–206 at y 194, running off the bottom of the live area.
  - Far end spans x 206–224 at y 140.
- Pilings: five short vertical posts under each side edge of the deck. They hang about 10 units below the deck at the near end, shrinking to about 3 at the far end.
- End post: one taller post at the far left corner of the deck, at (206, 140), rising 9 units above the deck.
- Water and sky: the rest of the card is empty texture.

LIGHT:

- Sky: a very faint band just above the horizon, about 0.15, near x 200–300 only, fading upward into background by y 80.
- Reflection streaks: 0.6 at the top, dimming to 0.2 at the bottom, with gaps between the streaks so the column reads as ripple.

- Pilings: 0.25.
- Water elsewhere: left at background, the darkest area of the card, especially the lower right and the space between pier and reflection. That empty gap is the point.
- Upper sky and corners: background.

LINES:

- Pier deck: both long side edges full strength, solid; the far end edge full strength, solid.
- Plank seams: three or four cross lines on the deck, faint, solid, spaced closer together toward the far end.

- Reflection: none; it stays as dots only.
- Sightline: one faint dashed line from the top of the end post to the far light, the gaze across the water.

ORANGE: The far light's core and the inner glow out to radius about 12, plus the top two reflection streaks. The eye should land on the thing longed for, small and far away, before it finds the pier that can't reach it.
