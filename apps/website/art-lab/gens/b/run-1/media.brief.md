TITLE: A ray of light bending as it passes through a glass slab

IDEA: A medium is what a message travels through, and like a ray entering glass, every message is bent and shifted by the material it passes through, arriving parallel to where it started but never quite where it would have landed.

COMPOSITION: A rectangular glass slab spanning x 115–285 and y 105–155 (170 wide, 50 tall), centred on the card. The incoming ray runs from (100, 60) to its entry point on the slab's top face at (170, 105). Inside the glass it bends steeper, from (170, 105) to its exit point on the bottom face at (186, 155). The outgoing ray leaves parallel to the incoming one, from (186, 155) to (228, 182). A ghost path continues the original, undeviated direction from (170, 105) to (248, 155). A weak reflected ray leaves the entry point from (170, 105) to (226, 69). Small bright points mark the entry and exit, each about 4 units across.
