TITLE: A glass prism fanning a single beam onto a measuring screen

IDEA: Newton's prism is science in one gesture: take something everyone thinks is simple, put it through an instrument, and find hidden structure you can measure.

- An equilateral-ish triangular prism, apex up, with vertices at (200, 72), (150, 170) and (250, 170).
- An incoming beam, a narrow band about 6 units thick, running from (90, 112) to the prism's left face at (175, 121).
- An inner path inside the glass from (175, 121) to the right face at (227, 126).
- A fan of five rays leaving the right face at (227, 126) and ending on a screen at x = 300. They land at y = 124, 136, 148, 160 and 172.
- The screen is a thin upright from (300, 104) to (300, 190).
- A small landing spot of radius 3 at (300, 148), where the middle ray strikes.
- A faint ground line beneath the prism at y = 176, running from x 120 to 320.
