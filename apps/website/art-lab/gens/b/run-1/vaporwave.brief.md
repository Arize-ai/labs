TITLE: Sliced sun sinking behind an endless wireframe floor

IDEA: Vaporwave's core image is a sunset that never finishes over a grid that leads nowhere, a nostalgic simulation of a future that never arrived.

COMPOSITION:
- Horizon: a straight horizontal line at y 140, running from x 84 to 316.
- Sun: a disc of radius 54 centred at (200, 128). Only the part above the horizon is printed, so it shows as a tall dome standing on the line.
- Sun slices: four horizontal gaps cut through the lower half of the dome. They sit at y 110, 119, 127 and 134, and are 2, 3, 4 and 5 units thick. They get thicker as they near the horizon, like blinds.
- Halo: a soft ring of light around the dome, reaching out to radius about 85 from (200, 128). It is clipped at the horizon.
- Floor: a perspective grid filling the card below the horizon, from y 140 down to 194.
  - Nine straight rails fan out from a vanishing point at (200, 140). They meet the bottom of the area at evenly spaced x positions from 84 to 316, about 29 apart.
  - Horizontal rungs cross the rails at y 143, 147, 153, 162, 175 and 194. The gaps widen toward the viewer.
- Sky: empty above the halo.

LIGHT:
- Sun dome: tone 0.95, nearly kissing dots. It shades very slightly, to about 0.85, toward its lower right, away from the upper-left light.
- Slices: tone 0, fully dark gaps, so the bands read crisply.
- Halo: tone 0.35 right at the sun's rim, falling off to 0 by radius 85, as shrinking dots.
- Floor: a faint glow along the horizon, tone 0.3 at y 141, fading to 0.05 by y 175. Below that it is just background texture.
- The sky above y 60 and the card corners stay dark, untouched texture.

LINES:
- Horizon: full, solid.
- Floor rails: half, solid. They fade to faint in the outermost two on each side.
- Floor rungs: half, solid near the horizon, becoming faint and dashed for the two rungs nearest the viewer (y 175 and 194).
- Sun outline: a faint, solid arc tracing the dome's edge above the horizon, so the silhouette holds at thumbnail size.

ORANGE: The sun dome, slices included, is printed in orange. It is the single glowing object the whole scene points to: every rail converges beneath it, so the eye lands there first. The halo and floor glow stay in the main ink.

VARIATION:
- Jitter the slice positions by ±1.5 units and their thicknesses by ±1 unit, so each sun is sliced a little differently.
- Shift all floor rungs together by a random offset of 0 to 1 spacing, so the grid looks caught at a different moment of scrolling toward the viewer.
