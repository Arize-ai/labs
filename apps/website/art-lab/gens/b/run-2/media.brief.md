TITLE: A ray of light bending as it passes through a glass slab

IDEA: A medium is whatever a message travels through, and this plate shows the medium bending the signal and handing it on shifted from where it would have gone.

COMPOSITION: A horizontal rectangular glass slab spans x 110–290 and y 105–150, 180 wide and 45 thick, centred at (200, 128). A light source is a small point at (95, 62), with a glow of radius about 18 around it. The incident ray runs from (95, 62) to the entry point (170, 105) on the slab's top face. Inside the glass the refracted segment runs more steeply from (170, 105) to the exit point (200, 150) on the bottom face. The emergent ray leaves (200, 150) parallel to the incident ray and runs to (275, 193). A ghost path continues the incident direction straight through the slab from (170, 105) to (248, 150), showing where the light would have gone without the medium. A weak reflected ray bounces off the top face from (170, 105) up to (235, 68).

LIGHT:
- **Source:** tone 1 at its core, with a glow falling off to 0 by radius 18.
- **Incident ray:** a narrow glow band about 6 wide, tone 0.7 at its centre and fading to 0 at its edges.
- **Slab:** an even mid-tone of 0.35. It brightens to 0.55 in a band about 6 deep along the top face, which catches the upper-left light. It darkens to 0.25 toward the lower right corner.
- **Inside the slab:** a soft glow of about 0.6 along the refracted segment, spreading slightly into the glass.
- **Emergent ray:** a dimmer glow band of 0.45, losing some energy to the medium.
- **Reflected ray:** a very faint glow of 0.2.
- **Stays dark:** everything else is untouched background texture, including all the space below the slab to the left of the emergent ray and all the space above the slab to the right of the reflection.

LINES:
- The slab outline, all four edges: full, solid.
- The incident ray along its centre: full, solid.
- The emergent ray: half, solid.
- The reflected ray: faint, solid.
- The ghost straight-through path: faint, dashed.
- Short surface normals, vertical lines about 30 long, centred on the entry point (170, 105) and the exit point (200, 150): faint, dashed.

ORANGE: The refracted segment inside the glass, from (170, 105) to (200, 150), drawn as a solid line over its glow. This is the moment the medium acts on the message, so the eye should land there first.

VARIATION:
1. The angle of incidence varies by about ±6°. The entry, exit and emergent points move consistently with it, with the emergent ray always kept parallel to the incident ray.
2. The slab's glass tone varies dot by dot by about ±0.05, giving a slight seeded mottle, as if each pane were poured differently.
