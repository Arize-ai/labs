TITLE: A brass refractor on its tripod, aimed at one distant star

IDEA: A telescope is the instrument for seeing what is too far or faint for the naked eye, which is what observability does for a running system, and here it is shown at the moment of pointing at its target.

COMPOSITION:
- **Main tube:** a long, slightly tapered cylinder on a diagonal rising to the upper right. Its axis runs from the eyepiece end at (132, 166) to the objective end at (262, 98), about 147 units long. It is 18 units thick at the eyepiece end and 24 at the objective end.
- **Dew shield:** a short, wider collar on the front of the tube, 28 units thick and 22 long, ending at (272, 93).
- **Front lens:** an ellipse 6 wide and 28 tall, tilted to match the tube, at (273, 92).
- **Eyepiece:** a small stub 10 long and 8 thick, angling down from (126, 170) toward (118, 178).
- **Finder scope:** a thin parallel tube 6 thick, running from (190, 120) to (232, 98) and riding about 12 units above the main tube on two short struts.
- **Mount:** a small block 16×12 under the tube's balance point, centred at (198, 142).
- **Tripod:** three legs from the mount down to feet at (152, 190), (204, 193) and (250, 190).
- **Target star:** a point source at (300, 66).
- **Horizon:** a faint horizontal line across the card at y 190.

LIGHT:
- **Main tube:** a mid-tone of 0.55 with a cylindrical shade. It is brightest at 0.8 along its upper-left edge, where it faces the light, and falls to 0.25 on its lower-right underside.
- **Dew shield:** a little brighter than the tube, at 0.65.
- **Front lens:** dark at 0.15, with a crescent highlight of 0.7 on its upper-left rim.
- **Eyepiece and finder scope:** both 0.45 with the same top-lit shading.
- **Mount:** 0.4.
- **Tripod legs:** carried by lines only, with no dots beyond a faint 0.15.
- **Target star:** the brightest mass on the card, a tight core at full 1.0 inside a radial glow that falls to nothing by radius 28.
- **Faint second glow:** a very soft 0.12 glow around the front lens, as if starlight were being gathered.
- **Stays dark:** the sky, the ground below the horizon and all corners are left as bare background texture.

LINES:
- **Full solid:** the outline of the main tube and dew shield, and the rim of the front lens.
- **Half solid:** the eyepiece and finder outlines, the mount block, the finder struts, and the three tripod legs (half solid, each with a small foot tick).
- **Faint solid:** the horizon at y 190.
- **Faint dashed:** the line of sight running from the front lens centre (273, 92) to the star (300, 66).

ORANGE: The star's core and its glow are the only orange on the card. The whole instrument points at that spot, so the eye should land where the telescope is looking.

VARIATION:
- Jitter the star's position by ±8 units and its glow radius between 22 and 34.
- Scatter 3 to 6 tiny faint pinprick stars, at tone 0.2 in main ink, at random spots in the upper sky between y 56 and y 110, away from the tube.
