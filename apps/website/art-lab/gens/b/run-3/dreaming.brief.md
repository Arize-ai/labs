TITLE: A dark house on still water whose reflection has lit windows

IDEA: The house above the waterline is asleep and unlit, but its mirror image glows from within, the way a dreaming mind is lit inside while the body lies dark.

COMPOSITION:
- **Horizon:** a waterline at y 128, running from x 92 to x 308.
- **House:** a simple gabled house centred on x 200, sitting on the waterline.
  - Walls are a rectangle from x 166 to 234 and y 94 to 128.
  - The roof is a triangle from (160, 94) to an apex at (200, 68) to (240, 94).
  - A small chimney spans x 218 to 226 and y 72 to 84.
  - Two square windows, 12 units wide, are centred at (183, 110) and (217, 110).
- **Reflection:** an exact mirror of the house below the waterline.
  - Walls run from y 128 to 162.
  - The roof apex is at (200, 188).
  - The windows are centred at (183, 146) and (217, 146).
- **Water glow:** a soft oval glow around the reflection, about 60 wide and 28 tall, centred at (200, 150).

LIGHT:
- **Upper house:** stays dark. Its walls and roof are tone 0.05, barely above the background texture, and its windows are tone 0. It is read only by its outline.
- **Sky:** left completely alone.
- **Reflected walls:** tone 0.25, shading to 0.15 toward the roof apex, so the image fades as it goes deeper.
- **Reflected windows:** tone 1, the brightest mass on the card.
  - Each has a glow falling off to tone 0.4 at radius 14, then to nothing by radius 30.
  - The glow spills across the reflected wall and onto the surrounding water.
- **Water around the reflection:** tone 0.1 near the house, dropping to background by x 120 and x 280.
- **Stays dark:** everything above the waterline except the outline, and the water beyond the glow.

LINES:
- **Waterline:** full strength, solid, x 92 to 308.
- **Upper house outline** (walls, roof, chimney, window frames): full strength, solid.
- **Reflected house outline:** half strength, dashed, so it reads as a mirrored image rather than a second house.
- **Ripples:** three or four short horizontal strokes, faint, solid, 20 to 50 units long. They lie across the reflection at y 140, 152, 165 and 178, slightly offset left and right of centre.

ORANGE: The reflected left window at (183, 146) and its inner glow are orange. It is the one point of warmth on the card, the place the dream happens, and it sits just below centre where the eye drops naturally from the dark house above.

VARIATION:
- Vary the position, length and count of the ripple strokes (3 to 5, lengths 20 to 50, x offsets within ±20).
- Let the reflected outline wobble horizontally by up to 1.5 units, as if the water were faintly moving.
