TITLE: A single fern frond uncurling into a fiddlehead spiral

IDEA: A fern grows by repeating itself, each leaflet a smaller copy of the whole, and it unrolls from a tight coil; that makes it a living picture of recursion and of things unfolding step by step.

COMPOSITION: A gently curving midrib rises from (150, 192) through (185, 145) and (225, 100) and ends where it rolls into a crozier, a tightened spiral about 1.75 turns long, centred at (258, 84) with an outer radius of 24 that shrinks to a tight core of radius 4. Eleven pairs of leaflets (pinnae) branch alternately from the midrib at about 50° to it. Each is a slender lanceolate blade, 46 long and 9 wide at the base pair, shrinking steadily to 12 long and 3 wide just below the coil. The left-side pinnae reach toward x ≈ 100 and the right-side ones toward x ≈ 230. Each of the six lowest pinnae carries 5–7 small rounded lobes (pinnules) along both edges, 4–6 units across, which repeats the frond's shape at small scale. The last three pinnae before the coil are only half-opened, curled stubs tucked against the stem. Nothing else is on the card.

LIGHT: The pinnae are mid-tone, 0.55 at their centres. Each is shaded across its width: 0.7 on the upper-left edge and 0.35 on the lower-right edge, so every blade reads as slightly folded along its vein. Tone also fades along the frond, from 0.5 at the base pinnae up to 0.75 near the coil, so the frond brightens as it rises. The midrib is a narrow band at 0.6. The crozier spiral is the brightest mass, its coiled band at 0.9. A soft glow surrounds it, starting at 0.4 at the coil's edge and falling to nothing at a radius of about 55 from (258, 84). Below the frond's base, the bottom 10 units of stem fade from 0.5 to 0. The background is left untouched everywhere: no ground, no other plants, and the spaces between pinnae stay at base texture.

LINES: The midrib from base into the spiral, traced in a full solid line that follows the coil to its centre. The outline of every pinna, in a half solid line. The central vein of each pinna, in a faint solid line. The outer edge of the crozier spiral's band, doubled as a second half solid line so the coil reads at thumbnail size. One faint dashed line continuing the spiral's curve outward by a further quarter-turn beyond the outer coil, as a hint of where it will unroll.

ORANGE: The innermost core of the fiddlehead, the last tight half-turn of the spiral (radius 4–8 around (258, 84)), printed at full tone. It is the growing tip where the next leaflet is still being formed, so it is the eye's natural landing point and the heart of the idea.

VARIATION: Vary the angle of each pinna by ±6° and its length by ±10% so the frond looks grown rather than drawn. Rotate the crozier's start angle by up to ±20° so the coil sits a little differently on each print.
