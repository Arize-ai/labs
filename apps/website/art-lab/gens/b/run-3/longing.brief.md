TITLE: A jetty that stops short of a distant light across still water

IDEA: Longing is reaching toward something you can see but cannot get to. The jetty points straight at the far light and ends in open water, so the gap between them is the subject.

COMPOSITION:
- **Horizon:** a straight horizontal line at y 100, running from x 84 to x 316.
- **Distant light:** a single small point just above the horizon at (250, 97), radius 3.
- **Glow:** a soft circular glow around the distant light, radius about 34.
- **Reflection:** a vertical streak below the light on the water. It is centred on x 250 and runs from y 104 down to y 132. It is about 8 wide at the top and narrows to about 3, broken into four or five short horizontal dashes like ripples.
- **Jetty deck:** a perspective trapezoid whose sides converge toward the distant light. The near end spans x 160–210 at y 194. The far end spans x 214–234 at y 138.
- **Posts:** a row of short vertical stubs under each side edge of the deck, every 10–12 units along it. They are about 6 tall at the near end and 2 tall at the far end.
- **The gap:** the empty water between the jetty's end (y 138) and the reflection's tip (y 132), and on up to the light. Nothing else is in it.

LIGHT:
- **Distant light:** the brightest mass, tone 1.
- **Glow:** tone 0.6 next to the light, fading to nothing by radius 34. It spreads a little more along the horizon than vertically.
- **Reflection streak:** tone 0.7 at the top, fading to 0.25 at its tip so it dies just before the jetty.
- **Jetty deck:** tone 0.45 at the near end, dimming to 0.25 at the far end, so the eye travels outward along it. The left half of the deck is a step brighter than the right, since light comes from the upper left.
- **Posts:** tone 0.2.
- **Kept dark:** the sky and all the water outside the glow and streak stay at bare background texture. In particular, the gap between the jetty's end and the reflection must stay empty.

LINES:
- **Horizon:** half strength, solid, broken for about 20 units on either side of the light so the glow reads cleanly.
- **Jetty deck:** both side edges and the far end edge in full strength, solid. The near edge is left open where it runs off toward the card edge.
- **Posts:** faint, solid, short verticals.
- **Ripples:** two or three faint horizontal dashes on the water just beyond the jetty's end, each 10–20 wide, between y 134 and y 146.
- **Sightline:** a faint dashed line from the centre of the jetty's far end (224, 138) to the light (250, 97). It is the unreachable path, drawn with very wide gaps so it is felt more than seen.

ORANGE: The distant light and the top third of its reflection. It is the thing being longed for, so the eye lands there first and then follows the jetty back toward the viewer.

VARIATION:
- **Ripple dashes:** vary their count (two to four) and their exact positions and widths on each print.
- **Reflection breaks:** vary where the streak breaks into dashes and its exact tip length (y 128–134), so the water never looks quite the same twice.
