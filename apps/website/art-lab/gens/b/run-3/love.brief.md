TITLE: Binary star pair circling the empty point they share

IDEA: Two stars bound so closely that each orbits not the other but a common centre neither occupies, which makes love the invisible point both lives turn around.

COMPOSITION: The shared centre (barycentre) sits at (200, 125). The larger star is a disc of radius 20 centred at (165, 125). The smaller star is a disc of radius 12 centred at (258, 125), so they sit on opposite sides of the centre, the heavier one closer to it. Each star rides its own orbit, both seen slightly from above as flattened ellipses centred on (200, 125). The inner orbit, for the large star, has semi-axes 35 × 12. The outer orbit, for the small star, has semi-axes 58 × 20. A soft glow surrounds each star: radius about 55 around the large star and 38 around the small one. The two glows overlap in a faint luminous bridge between x 180 and 240. Three pinprick background stars sit at (112, 78), (291, 171) and (238, 66), each radius 1.5. Nothing else is on the card.

LIGHT:
- Large star core: 1.0, solid full-size dots, with a slight bias toward the upper left so the lower right rim drops to 0.85.
- Small star core: 0.9, same treatment.
- Large star glow: falls off from 0.7 at the rim to 0 at radius 55, fading fast at first, then lingering.
- Small star glow: falls off from 0.6 at the rim to 0 at radius 38.
- Bridge: where the glows overlap, add their light so a band about 0.3 forms between the stars. It should brighten slightly toward the centre point but never reach it as a solid. Directly around (200, 125), within radius 6, stay at 0.15 so the orange point sits in a small hollow.
- Background stars: 0.5, single enlarged dots.
- Everything else stays as bare background texture, including the space above and below the orbits and all corners.

LINES:
- Inner orbit ellipse: half strength, dashed. Break it where it passes behind the large star's disc on the upper arc.
- Outer orbit ellipse: half strength, dashed. Break it behind the small star on the upper arc.
- Each star core gets a crisp outline circle at full strength, solid, at radius 20 and 12.
- At the centre point, a tiny crosshair of two faint solid strokes, 10 units long each way, horizontal and vertical.
- A single faint solid line joins the two star centres, passing through the centre point. Hide it under the star discs.

ORANGE: The barycentre point is a small orange dot of radius 3 at (200, 125), sitting inside its crosshair. It is the one place that holds the whole system together, yet nothing lives there. The eye should go past both bright stars and settle on the small orange heart of their relationship.

VARIATION:
1. Rotate the whole system around (200, 125) by a random angle between −18° and +18°, keeping the stars opposite each other on the line through the centre.
2. Randomise the orbital phase by sliding both stars along their ellipses by the same small random amount, so they still face each other through the centre. Also jitter the background star positions by up to ±10 units.
