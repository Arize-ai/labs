TITLE: A ray of light bending through a glass slab

IDEA: A medium is whatever a signal passes through, and the bend a ray takes inside the glass shows that the carrier always changes the message.

COMPOSITION:
- A rectangular glass slab, axis-aligned, spanning x 120–280 and y 105–165 (160 wide, 60 tall), centred at (200, 135).
- A small point source at (96, 62), drawn as a bright disc of radius 5.
- An incoming ray from the source to an entry point on the slab's top face at (170, 105).
- A refracted segment inside the slab, running more steeply from (170, 105) to an exit point on the bottom face at (200, 165).
- An outgoing ray from (200, 165) to (246, 192), parallel to the incoming ray but offset sideways.
- A short, weak reflected ray leaving the entry point up and to the right, from (170, 105) to about (205, 85).
- Two short surface normals, perpendicular to the faces: one through the entry point (x 170, y 85–125) and one through the exit point (x 200, y 145–185).

LIGHT:
- Point source: tone 1.0, with a generous round glow of radius about 30 falling off to nothing. This is the brightest mass on the card.
- Incoming ray: a narrow band of light about 6 units wide at tone 0.7, with a soft one-dot falloff on either side.
- Slab body: tone 0.3 at its upper-left corner, shrinking to 0.12 at its lower-right, so it reads as a lit block of glass. Add a slightly brighter strip at 0.45, about 4 units deep, along the top face where the light enters.
- Inside the slab, a faint haze of tone 0.2 around the refracted segment, as if the glass is scattering a little light.
- Outgoing ray: tone 0.45 and fading toward its end, since the light has lost something in the glass.
- Reflected ray: tone 0.2, barely above the texture.
- Everything else stays as bare background lattice, especially the upper right and the lower left.

LINES:
- Slab outline: all four edges, half strength, solid. The top face is full strength, solid.
- Incoming ray centreline: full strength, solid.
- Refracted segment inside the glass: full strength, solid (it prints in orange).
- Outgoing ray centreline: half strength, solid.
- Reflected ray: faint, dashed.
- Both surface normals: faint, dashed.
- Optionally, a faint dashed continuation of the incoming ray's original straight path through the slab, from (170, 105) to about (222, 135), so the deviation is visible.

ORANGE: The refracted segment inside the slab, from (170, 105) to (200, 165), both its line and its small band of dots. This is where the medium acts on the light, and the kink in the path is the whole idea of the plate.

VARIATION:
1. The slab's tilt: rotate it by a random angle between −6° and +6° about its centre, then recompute the entry, exit and outgoing points so the refraction stays physically consistent.
2. The brightness and size of the scattering haze inside the glass: tone between 0.12 and 0.28 and width between 8 and 16 units, so some prints read as clear glass and others as cloudier.
