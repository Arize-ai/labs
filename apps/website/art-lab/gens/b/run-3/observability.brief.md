TITLE: Cloud chamber viewed from above, particle tracks crossing its glowing mist

IDEA: You never see the particle itself, only the trail it leaves behind, and observability is exactly this: knowing a hidden system by the traces it writes.

COMPOSITION:
- **Chamber window:** a circular glass disc of radius 64, centred at (200, 125).
- **Outer bezel:** a ring of radius 72, concentric with the window.
- **Long track:** a thin straight track running from the rim at about (148, 87) to the rim at about (258, 152).
- **Alpha track:** a short, fat straight track, 30 long, starting at the rim near (147, 155) and heading up-right toward (172, 140).
- **Faint track:** a straight track crossing near the bottom, from (175, 183) to (240, 175).
- **Spiral track:** enters from the rim near (252, 88), bends inward and curls into a tightening spiral of about three turns. The spiral is centred at (214, 118), with its outer turn at radius 16 and its inner turn at radius 3.
- **Background:** nothing outside the bezel.

LIGHT:
- **Mist:** tone 0.25, falling smoothly to 0.12 at the lower right, as if lit by a lamp at the upper left. Its edge is held firm at the glass rim.
- **Long track:** tone 0.85 along its core, with a narrow glow two to three dots wide that fades to the mist tone.
- **Alpha track:** tone 1.0 and about 5 units thick, with a wider glow of about 8 units. This is the brightest mass on the plate.
- **Faint track:** tone 0.5, with no glow.
- **Spiral track:** tone 0.9, with a soft glow that pools slightly at the spiral's centre.
- **Bezel band:** the band between radius 64 and 72 stays at tone 0.08.
- **Dark areas:** everything outside the bezel is left as plain background texture.

LINES:
- **Window rim:** circle of radius 64, full, solid.
- **Bezel outer edge:** circle of radius 72, half, solid.
- **Long track:** a centre line along its length, half, solid.
- **Faint track:** a centre line, faint, dashed, so it reads as broken droplets.
- **Spiral track:** traced along its whole path, full, solid.
- **Alpha track:** no line; its dot mass carries it.

ORANGE: The spiral track and its glow, line and dots together. It is the one trail that curls and tells a story (charge, energy, a particle losing speed), so the eye should land there first, the way an engineer's eye lands on the one anomalous trace.

VARIATION:
- **Straight tracks:** vary their angles and entry points by up to ±15° and ±10 units, and add zero to two extra faint dashed tracks.
- **Spiral:** vary its centre by up to ±8 units, its number of turns between 2.5 and 3.5, and its direction of curl.
