TITLE: Sliced sunset sinking behind an endless wireframe grid floor

IDEA: Vaporwave is nostalgia for a digital future that never came: a striped sun forever setting over a perfect, empty, computer-drawn plain that leads nowhere.

COMPOSITION: A horizon at y 140, running x 84–316. A sun disc of radius 48 centred at (200, 128), cut off by the horizon so only the part above y 140 shows: a dome rising to y 80 and 92 units wide at the horizon. Four horizontal slices are cut across the sun's lower half, each a band of bare background:
- y 110, 1.5 tall
- y 119, 2.5 tall
- y 127, 3.5 tall
- y 134, 4.5 tall

The bands thicken toward the horizon, like venetian blinds. Below the horizon, a perspective grid floor fills y 140–194 and fades out at the card edges. Its vanishing point is (200, 140). Eleven radial lines run from it to the bottom edge at x = 40, 72, 104, 136, 168, 200, 232, 264, 296, 328 and 360. Six cross lines sit at y 143, 147, 153, 161, 172 and 188, spaced closer together toward the horizon. On the floor, a narrow reflection of the sun lies directly below it: a column 30 wide at the horizon, tapering to 12 wide at y 180.

LIGHT:
- Sun disc: tone 1.0, the brightest mass. It is flat-full at the top and eases very slightly to 0.85 toward the horizon. The slices are left completely dark.
- Glow: tone 0.35 at the sun's rim, falling off in dot size to nothing by radius 85. It lights the sky above the horizon only. The horizon cuts it sharp, with no glow below the line.
- Reflection: tone 0.3 at the horizon, fading to 0 by y 180. It is broken into horizontal dashes that echo the slices.
- Grid floor: no fill between the lines, only a very faint 0.08 lift just under the horizon, dying out by y 155.
- Stays dark: the sky beyond the glow, the whole upper card, and everything outside the grid's fan.

LINES:
- Horizon: full, solid, from x 84 to 316.
- Radial grid lines: half strength, solid. They fade to faint toward the card edges.
- Cross grid lines: half strength, solid. The two nearest the horizon (y 143, 147) drop to faint so the distance reads.
- Sun outline: faint, solid, tracing the visible dome above the horizon.
- Slice edges: no lines. The cuts are carried by the absence of dots alone.

ORANGE: The sun disc and its reflection on the floor. The sun is the one warm, glowing thing in a cold, empty synthetic world, and the slices through it are the genre's signature. Orange wins over the grid lines wherever they cross the reflection.

VARIATION: Vary how far the sun has sunk (centre y between 122 and 134), so each print catches a different moment of the endless sunset. Vary the slice positions by ±1.5 units each.
