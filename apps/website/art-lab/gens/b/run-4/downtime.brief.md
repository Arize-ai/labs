TITLE: A lighthouse with its lamp dark, set against a rising moon

IDEA: Downtime is the moment a thing built to shine goes quiet. A lighthouse with an unlit lantern reads instantly as "out of service" without resorting to a stopped clock or a flatline.

COMPOSITION:
- Horizon: a straight sea line across the card at y 170, from x 90 to x 310.
- Moon: a full disc of radius 40 centred at (232, 104), sitting just behind and to the right of the tower.
- Tower: a tapered shaft centred on x 178. It is 34 wide at its base on the horizon (x 161–195, y 170) and narrows to 22 wide at y 94 (x 167–189).
- Gallery: a thin platform 30 wide (x 163–193) at y 92 with a low rail 4 tall above it.
- Lantern room: a box 18 wide and 16 tall (x 169–187, y 72–88), divided into three vertical glazing panes.
- Dome: a small domed cap from y 72 rising to a point at (178, 62), with a short finial to y 58.
- Rocks: a low irregular mound 70 wide and about 10 tall at the tower's foot, x 143–213, y 160–170.
- Sea: a moon-path of short broken horizontal strokes on the water directly below the moon, centred on x 232. It is about 40 wide near the horizon and widens to about 70 by y 192.

LIGHT:
- Moon: the brightest mass at tone 0.9. Shade it slightly brighter at the upper left (0.95) down to 0.8 at the lower right. Give it a soft glow falling from 0.4 at the rim to nothing about 45 units out, so the sky around it shimmers with shrinking dots.
- Tower, gallery, lantern and dome: tone 0, held fully dark as a silhouette against the moon and its glow. The lantern panes especially must stay empty. The absence of light there is the whole point.
- Rocks: tone 0.1, with a faint 0.2 catch of moonlight along their upper-right edges.
- Moon-path on the water: tone 0.5 near the horizon, shrinking to 0.15 at the bottom, broken into horizontal dashes rather than a continuous fill.
- The rest of the sea and sky: left as bare background texture.

LINES:
- Tower outline: both tapered sides and the base. Full, solid.
- Gallery platform and rail: full, solid.
- Lantern room: box outline and the two pane dividers, full, solid. The dome curve and finial are also full and solid.
- Horizon line: half strength, solid, running x 90–310 and interrupted where the tower and rocks stand.
- Rock silhouette top edge: half strength, solid.
- Moon rim: faint, solid, so the disc holds its shape in a thumbnail.
- A handful of swell lines on the sea outside the moon-path, 20–40 long, scattered between y 176 and 190: faint, dashed.

ORANGE: A single tiny ember, a dot cluster about 3 units across, at the centre of the lantern room (178, 81). It is the last glow of a cooling filament. The eye lands where the beam should be and finds only a spark, which is exactly what downtime feels like.

VARIATION:
- Jitter the position, length and spacing of the moon-path dashes and the sea swell lines, so each print's water breaks differently.
- Vary the moon's vertical position by ±8 units and the ember's brightness slightly, so some prints feel a moment earlier or later in the outage.
