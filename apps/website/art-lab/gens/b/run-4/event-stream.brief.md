TITLE: Paper lanterns drifting single file down a winding night river

IDEA: An event stream is a stream of discrete, ordered happenings carried past an observer, and lanterns floating downstream show exactly that: separate lights, one after another, all moving the same way and arriving in sequence.

COMPOSITION: A faint horizon at y 66 spans x 110–290. A river rises from that horizon as a narrow mouth between x 250 and 276. It swings left in a gentle S, about 50 wide at (205, 120) between x 180 and 236. It then swings back right and widens to about 150 at the foot of the plate, spanning x 150–300 at y 194. Eight lanterns float along the river's centreline. Each is a small rounded square, shrinking with distance:

- (244, 84), size 6
- (228, 96), size 7
- (213, 110), size 9
- (206, 126), size 11
- (212, 144), size 14
- (226, 162), size 17

Under each lantern hangs a short vertical reflection, about 1.5 times the lantern's height. Scattered across the water are a few short horizontal ripple flecks, each 6–15 long.

LIGHT:
- Lantern cores are tone 1, the brightest marks on the card.
- Each lantern has a round glow out to about three times its size. The glow falls from 0.6 at the lantern's edge to nothing, so the nearer lanterns throw larger pools of light.
- Reflections are columns of dots fading from 0.45 just under each lantern to 0 at their tips.
- The water is tone 0.12, barely above the background texture. It warms to about 0.25 inside each lantern's pool.
- The ripple flecks are 0.3 and appear only where they cross a glow.
- The banks, the land beyond them and the sky above the horizon are left as untouched background texture, so they stay dark.

LINES:
- Both riverbanks, traced from the horizon down to the bottom of the plate: half strength, solid.
- The horizon: faint, solid.
- The river's centreline, linking the lanterns in order like a timeline: faint, dashed.
- A thin rim round each of the four nearest lanterns: full strength, solid.

ORANGE: The sixth lantern at (212, 144), its core and its reflection in signal orange. It is the one event being inspected, caught mid-stream and halfway between where the stream came from and where it is going.

VARIATION: Jitter each lantern's position along and across the current by up to ±4 units and its size by ±15%, so the spacing feels like real, irregular event timing. Randomise the number, length and placement of the ripple flecks on every print.
