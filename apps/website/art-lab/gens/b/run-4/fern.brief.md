TITLE: A single fiddlehead fern uncurling, studied like a botanical plate

IDEA: The fiddlehead is the fern before it unfolds, a tight coil holding the whole frond in miniature, so it carries the fern's self-similarity and quiet growth more freshly than a full open leaf would.

COMPOSITION: A thick logarithmic-spiral crozier, about 2.5 turns, with its outer edge reaching radius 50 around a coil centre at (218, 108). The coil's body is a band about 16 wide on the outer turn, tapering to about 4 wide at the innermost turn. The stem leaves the outer turn at its lower left, around (172, 128), and descends in a gentle S-curve to (186, 194). It is about 10 wide at the top and 7 wide at the bottom. Along the outside of the outer turn sit 9 small rounded bumps, each about 5–7 in diameter and spaced evenly. These are the tucked pinnae, and they shrink toward the coil's open end. Two tiny paired leaflet buds, about 6 long, sprout from the stem at y 150 and y 170, angled upward. A faint ground horizon runs from x 120 to x 290 at y 190. Everything else is empty texture.

LIGHT: The outer coil band is tone 0.7, rising to 0.85 on its upper-left edge and falling to 0.45 on its lower-right inner edge, so it reads as a rounded tube lit from the upper left. Tone climbs inward along the spiral, reaching 0.9 on the innermost turn. A soft glow of radius 30 surrounds the coil centre, starting at 0.35 just outside the coil and falling to 0 so it melts into the texture. The pinnae bumps are tone 0.75 on their upper-left halves and 0.5 on their lower-right halves. The stem is 0.55 at the top and fades to 0.3 at the ground, a little brighter along its left edge. The stem buds are 0.4. The gaps between spiral turns are left at background, as is everything outside the subject and its glow.

ORANGE: Only the innermost curl tip, the last quarter-turn of the spiral (about 8 units of the band at the very centre), is printed in orange. This is where the coil bottoms out and where new growth begins, so it is the heart of the image.

VARIATION:
- Vary the number of spiral turns between 2.25 and 2.75, and the S-curve of the stem by up to ±8 units sideways.
- Vary the size of each pinna bump by ±1.5, so each print feels hand-grown.
