TITLE: A single cosmos bloom on a curving stem, botanical plate

IDEA: A flower is a structure that opens outward to offer one small centre to the world, so the plate shows that opening plainly: petals radiating around a glowing heart.

COMPOSITION: Bloom centred at (200, 108), seen face-on with a slight tilt so it reads as an ellipse about 130 wide and 110 tall. Eight petals, each a long rounded teardrop about 52 long and 22 wide at its broadest. They attach at the centre and are evenly rotated, with the tips slightly notched. Centre disk is a circle of radius 13 at (200, 108), with a ring of tiny stamen dots just outside it at radius 16. The stem leaves from just below the bloom at (202, 150), passes behind the lower petals, and curves gently down and right to (214, 194), where it fades into the edge. One slender leaf branches off the stem at (206, 172), arcing left to a point at (172, 180), about 36 long and 9 wide.

LIGHT:
- Petals: about 0.75 at their upper-left faces, shading to about 0.45 on the lower-right petals and toward each petal's root, where they tuck under the disk. Each petal gets a faint lengthwise gradient, brightest two-thirds of the way out.
- Centre disk: the brightest mass at 1.0, carrying a soft glow that falls off over about 30 units across the inner petals.
- Stamen ring: 0.6.
- Stem: 0.35.
- Leaf: 0.3, brighter along its upper edge.
- Everything else stays untouched background texture, and no glow reaches beyond the petal tips.

LINES:
- Each petal outline: half strength, solid.
- One faint solid midrib line down the centre of each petal.
- Centre disk outline: full strength, solid.
- Stem: two parallel half-strength solid lines about 3 apart.
- Leaf: half-strength solid outline, with a faint solid midrib.
- A single faint dashed circle of radius 70 around the bloom centre, a quiet instrument-plate reference ring the petal tips just touch.

ORANGE: The centre disk and its glow. It is the heart of the flower and the brightest point, so the eye lands there and the petals lead outward from it.

VARIATION:
1. Jitter each petal's length (±6) and angle (±5°), and rotate the whole set randomly so no two blooms align.
2. Vary the stem's curvature slightly, and which side the leaf branches toward.
