TITLE: A rising line graph whose last point glows like a lit filament

IDEA: A line graph turns a series of separate measurements into one readable path, so the plate shows the line itself, with its newest point burning brightest as the reading that matters now.

- **Axes:** an L-shaped pair. The vertical axis runs from (96, 186) up to (96, 62). The horizontal axis runs from (96, 186) right to (308, 186).
- **Data line:** a polyline of nine vertices climbing left to right in uneven steps: (106, 162), (130, 150), (152, 157), (176, 130), (200, 139), (224, 110), (248, 121), (270, 92), (290, 78).
- **Area under the line:** the region between the polyline and the horizontal axis, spanning x 106–290.
- **Vertex markers:** a small dot of radius 2.5 at each of the first eight vertices.
- **Final point:** a disc of radius 4.5 at (290, 78), surrounded by a glow of radius about 22.
- **Gridlines:** three horizontal guides at y 146, 106 and 66, running from x 96 to x 304.

LIGHT:
- **Area under the line:** a vertical gradient. It starts at tone 0.55 just beneath the line and shrinks to 0.08 at the axis, so the bottom edge melts into the background texture. Because the line climbs, the right side of the area is taller and holds more light.
- **Vertex markers:** tone 0.9.
- **Final point:** tone 1, the brightest mass on the card.
- **Glow around the final point:** falls off from 0.7 at the disc's edge to 0 at radius 22. It is slightly stronger toward the upper left, where the light comes from.
- **Dark areas:** everything above the line and everything outside the axes stays as untouched background texture. The strip just above the polyline stays dark so that the line reads crisply against it.

LINES:
- **Data line:** the polyline traced in full strength, solid.
- **Horizontal axis:** full strength, solid.
- **Vertical axis:** half strength, solid.
- **Gridlines:** the three guides at y 146, 106 and 66, faint and dashed.
- **Drop line:** a short line from the final point (290, 78) straight down to the horizontal axis at (290, 186), faint and dashed, marking where the latest reading sits.

ORANGE: The final point and its glow are printed in orange. The newest measurement is where the trend is heading and the reason anyone looks at the graph, so the eye should land there first.
