TITLE: A jetty over still water, stopping short of a distant light

IDEA: Longing is the gap between where you can stand and what you can see; the jetty reaches toward the far light and ends in open water, leaving that gap empty.

COMPOSITION:
- Horizon: a straight line across the card at y 118, running x 84–316.
- Distant light: a small point of radius 2.5 at (262, 113), sitting just above the horizon. A soft halo of radius 34 surrounds it.
- Reflection: a vertical column of broken horizontal streaks on the water directly below the light, from y 121 down to y 186. It is about 6 wide at the top and widens to about 26 at the bottom, and is made of 8–10 short dashes stacked with gaps between them.
- Jetty: a plank walkway seen in perspective.
  - It starts wide at the bottom-left foreground, with its near end spanning (96, 194) to (150, 194).
  - It narrows as it recedes toward the upper right and ends abruptly at a far edge from (196, 140) to (208, 140).
  - Five pairs of posts drop below its two edges. They are short vertical strokes that shrink with distance, from about 14 tall at the front to 3 tall at the far end.
- The open water between the jetty's end (x ≈ 208) and the reflection column (x ≈ 250) is left completely empty.
- Sky above the horizon is empty.

LIGHT:
- Distant light core: tone 1.
- Halo: falls off from 0.7 at the core to 0 at radius 34, a classic glow. The halo spreads a little more along the horizon than vertically, as if seen through haze.
- Reflection streaks: these are the brightest mass. They are tone 0.85 near the horizon and fade to 0.35 at the bottom, each streak dimmer at its ends.
- Jetty deck: a mid-tone of 0.4 at the near end, fading to 0.2 at the far end. It is lit slightly more on its left edge, with light from the upper left.
- Posts: tone 0.15.
- Water: a very faint band of 0.1 just below the horizon, under the halo only.
- Everything else stays dark, background texture only: the sky, the open water left and right, and above all the gap between the jetty's end and the light.

LINES:
- Horizon: half strength, solid. It fades to faint where it passes outside the halo (beyond x 210 and x 300).
- Jetty's two long side edges: full strength, solid, converging toward the far end.
- Jetty's near and far cross-edges: full strength, solid. The far edge is the abrupt stop and should read crisply.
- Plank seams across the deck: 6–7 faint, solid lines, spaced closer together as they recede.
- Posts: half strength, solid vertical strokes.
- Reflection: a faint dashed vertical guide running down the centre of the column, from (262, 121) to (262, 186). It keeps the column legible at thumbnail size.

ORANGE: Only the distant light's core and its innermost halo, out to radius 5. The eye should land on the thing that is longed for: small, far away and warm. The rest of the halo and the reflection stay in the main ink, so the orange point sits alone.

VARIATION:
- Vary the reflection's streaks in length and horizontal offset, by ±4 units each, so the water ripples differently on every print.
- Vary the light's position along the horizon between x 250 and x 278. This keeps the gap from the jetty's end always at least 40 units.
