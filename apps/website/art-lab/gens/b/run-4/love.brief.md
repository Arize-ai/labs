TITLE: A binary star pair, the smaller pouring light into the larger

IDEA: Love is two bodies bound into one orbit, each pulling the other off its own path and giving itself across the gap, which a binary star system shows exactly without resorting to a heart.

COMPOSITION:
- A shared centre of mass (barycentre) at (192, 124), unmarked except by a tiny cross.
- Star A, the heavier one: a disc of radius 28 centred at (160, 122).
- Star B, the lighter one: a disc of radius 18 centred at (248, 128).
- Two orbit ellipses centred on the barycentre, tilted about 6° clockwise:
  - A's orbit with semi-axes 32 × 10.
  - B's orbit with semi-axes 56 × 18.
  - Each star sits on its own ellipse, on opposite sides of the barycentre.
- A mass-transfer stream, about 4 units thick: a curved ribbon leaving B's inner limb near (231, 125), bowing slightly upward through about (210, 114), and wrapping onto A's right limb near (186, 116). There it thins into a short partial ring hugging A's edge, about a quarter turn.
- Five or six pinprick background stars scattered sparsely between x 100–300 and y 70–180, kept well clear of the pair.

LIGHT:
- **Star A:** tone 1.0 at an upper-left highlight near (151, 113), falling to 0.75 at its lower-right limb so it reads as a sphere. A glow falls from 0.45 at its edge to nothing at radius 62.
- **Star B:** tone 0.85 at an upper-left highlight, falling to 0.6 at its limb. A glow falls from 0.35 to nothing at radius 40.
- **The gap between them:** where the two glows overlap, let them sum to about 0.3, so a faint luminous bridge joins the pair.
- **Stream:** full tone along its length, in orange.
- **Background stars:** tone 0.4–0.55, single dots only.
- **What stays dark:** everything outside the glows is left as bare texture. That includes the space inside B's orbit beyond the glow and the whole outer band of the card.

LINES:
- A's orbit ellipse: faint, dashed.
- B's orbit ellipse: faint, dashed.
- Limb outline of Star A: half, solid.
- Limb outline of Star B: half, solid.
- Barycentre cross, arms 4 units long: faint, solid.
- Edge of the stream ribbon: one full-strength solid line along its centre in orange, so the gesture survives at thumbnail size.

ORANGE: The stream of matter flowing from the small star into the large one. It is the act of giving, the thing that makes two stars a relationship rather than a coincidence, so the eye should land there first.

VARIATION:
- Rotate the pair's orbital phase around the barycentre by up to ±25°. Both stars stay on their ellipses and on opposite sides, and the stream re-routes between their facing limbs.
- Randomise the positions and tones of the pinprick background stars within their allowed band.
