TITLE: Pocket watch seen through its glass caseback, movement exposed

IDEA: Observability means seeing a system's inner state from outside, and an exhibition caseback lets you watch the whole movement tick without ever opening the case.

COMPOSITION:
- **Case:** a circle of radius 66 centred at (200, 132), seen flat from the back.
- **Crown:** a short ribbed stem, 14 wide and 10 tall, sitting on top of the case, spanning y 56–66, centred at x 200.
- **Glass:** an inner bezel ring of radius 58, same centre. The glass fills it.
- **Main gear:** radius 28 centred at (182, 142), with 24 short teeth and four spokes.
- **Escape wheel:** radius 15 centred at (228, 110), with sharp hooked teeth, meshing with the main gear's upper right.
- **Balance wheel:** a ring of radius 18 centred at (226, 160), with two crossbar spokes. A tight hairspring spiral of three or four turns sits at its hub.
- **Bridge plate:** a curved crescent, about 10 wide, arching from (160, 100) to (250, 186) and partly covering the gears. Three jewel bearings sit on it as dots of radius 3, at (182, 142), (228, 110) and (226, 160).
