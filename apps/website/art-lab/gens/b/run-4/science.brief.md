TITLE: A glass prism splitting one beam into a measured fan of rays

IDEA: Science is taking a single undifferentiated thing, light, and resolving it into parts you can see, sort and measure, the way Newton's prism made a white beam confess its spectrum.

COMPOSITION:
- A triangular prism standing on its base, with its apex at (200, 70) and base corners at (150, 170) and (250, 170).
- An incoming beam about 6 units thick that enters from the left at (90, 140) and strikes the prism's left face at (172, 126).
- Inside the glass, the beam runs from (172, 126) to the right face at (232, 135).
- From (232, 135) a fan of seven rays spreads to the right. They land evenly spaced on a vertical screen at x = 300, between y = 118 and y = 180.
- The screen is a narrow upright strip 4 units wide, running from (300, 95) to (300, 185).
- A shallow floor line runs under the prism at y = 172, from x = 120 to x = 280.

LIGHT:
- The incoming beam is the brightest mass, at tone 1.0 along its core. It has a tight glow that falls to 0.3 within 8 units of the core and is slightly stronger on its upper side, toward the upper-left light.
- The beam inside the glass sits at 0.8.
- The prism body is a faint mid-tone. It shades from 0.35 near the upper-left face to 0.15 toward the lower-right, so the glass reads as a solid lit from the upper left. A thin brighter band of 0.5 runs just inside the left face.
- The fan is a wedge of soft light whose tone drops from 0.6 at the exit point to 0.25 at the screen. The dots shrink outward along the spread.
- Each ray's landing spot on the screen is a small glow 3 units in radius at tone 0.7.
- The screen strip sits at 0.2.
- Everything else stays dark, leaving only the base texture. This includes the space above and below the fan, the left third of the card apart from the beam, and everything below the floor line.

LINES:
- The prism's three edges: full, solid.
- The incoming beam: two edge lines along it, half, solid.
- The beam inside the glass: one centreline, half, solid.
- The seven fan rays: each a separate line from the exit point to the screen, faint, solid.
- The screen: an outline, half, solid.
- The floor line: faint, dashed.
- The normal to the left face at the entry point, about 30 units long: faint, dashed, like the construction line in a textbook diagram.

ORANGE: Only one ray is orange, the fifth from the top, along with its 3-unit landing glow on the screen. It is the single observation picked out of the spectrum, and the eye should land on the point where measurement happens.

VARIATION:
- Vary the fan's spread a little each print. The landing span on the screen should range from roughly y 114–184 to y 122–176, and the ray count from 6 to 8.
- Choose which ray is orange at random, any of the middle rays but never the outermost two.
