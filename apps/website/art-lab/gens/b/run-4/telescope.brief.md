TITLE: A small refractor on a tripod, aimed at one bright star

IDEA: A telescope is the instrument that brings one faint, distant thing close enough to study, the way careful observation singles out a signal from a sky full of noise.

COMPOSITION:
- Horizon: a straight level ground line across the card at y = 188, from x 90 to x 310.
- Tripod: three legs meeting at a mount head at (178, 140). The left leg ends at (128, 188), the right leg at (226, 188), and the rear leg, half hidden, at (190, 186). The mount head is a small rounded block about 14 × 10 centred on (178, 140).
- Tube: a long cylinder whose axis runs from the eyepiece end at (122, 162) to the objective end at (262, 92), tilted up and to the right at about 27°, roughly 157 long.
  - It is 16 wide at the back and widens to 20 at the front.
  - It rests on the mount by a short saddle at (180, 133).
- Dew shield: a slightly wider sleeve, 24 across, over the last 30 units of the tube, ending in an open circular lip at (262, 92), drawn as an ellipse of 24 × 10 perpendicular to the axis.
- Eyepiece: a short stubby cylinder, 10 long and 8 wide, angled down off the back end near (116, 168). Add a small focuser knob at (134, 150).
- Finder scope: a thin parallel tube, 40 long and 6 wide, riding 10 units above the main tube, centred at (205, 110).
- Star: a point at (298, 66) with a four-ray diffraction cross, each ray about 14 long, and a round glow of radius 22.

LIGHT:
- Star: brightest point on the card, tone 1.0 at the core. The glow falls off over its 22-unit radius to background.
- Tube: modelled as a cylinder lit from the upper left.
  - The upper edge carries a narrow highlight band at tone 0.8.
  - The body sits in mid-tone 0.5.
  - The underside shades down to 0.2.
- Dew shield and lip: the upper rim of the lip catches 0.85. The inside of the opening stays dark at 0.1, which reads as the hollow bore.
- Finder, mount and eyepiece: mid-tones at 0.4–0.5, with the upper-left faces slightly lighter.
- Tripod legs: thin strokes of dots at tone 0.35, fading slightly toward the feet.
- Ground: a very faint band of 0.1 just above the horizon line, barely above the texture.
- Sky: an extremely faint wash of 0.08 widening from the dew shield toward the star. It should be almost invisible and only hint at the line of sight.
- Background: everything else is left as bare texture.

LINES:
- Tube outline (both long edges, the back end and the step where the dew shield begins): full, solid.
- Objective lip ellipse: full, solid.
- Finder scope outline: half, solid.
- Eyepiece, focuser knob and mount head outlines: half, solid.
- Tripod legs, as a single line along each: full for the two front legs, half for the rear leg, all solid.
- Horizon line: faint, solid.
- Line of sight from the centre of the objective at (262, 92) to the star: faint, dashed.

ORANGE: Only the star, meaning its core, the diffraction cross and its glow. Everything in the instrument points there, so the eye should travel up the tube and land on the one thing being watched.

VARIATION:
- Move the star's position within a small box, x 285–305 and y 60–74, and re-aim the tube's tilt and the dashed sight line to match.
- Scatter three to five tiny faint background stars, tone 0.2–0.3 in main ink, at random spots in the upper sky away from the main star.
