TITLE: Striped sun setting over an endless wireframe grid

IDEA: Vaporwave is nostalgia for a digital future that never arrived. The sliced sun sinking over an infinite neon floor is that dream frozen at the moment it sets.

COMPOSITION:
- Horizon: a straight line at y 140, spanning x 84–316.
- Sun: a disc of radius 52 centred at (200, 132), cut flat by the horizon. Only the part above y 140 shows, from y 80 down to the horizon.
- Sun stripes: the lower half of the visible disc is sliced by five horizontal gaps. Their centres sit at y 113, 121, 128, 134 and 138.5, and they are 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5 and 5 units tall, so they thicken toward the horizon.
- Floor rays: a perspective grid fills the area below the horizon (y 140–194). Its vanishing point is (200, 140). Eleven rays fan out from it and meet the bottom edge, y 194, at x 20, 60, 100, 140, 180, 200, 220, 260, 300, 340 and 380, clipped to the live area.
- Floor crossbars: horizontal grid lines at y 142.5, 146, 151, 158, 168 and 183, spreading apart as they come toward the viewer.
- Haze: a low band hugging the horizon, x 110–290, from y 140 to about y 152.

LIGHT:
- Sun disc: tone 1, a solid mass of kissing dots, slightly fuller at the upper left.
- Stripe gaps: tone 0, cut cleanly out of the sun.
- Glow: a halo around the sun starts at 0.5 at the rim and fades to 0 by radius 95. It is strongest above and to the left, and it does not reach below the horizon.
- Haze: 0.35 right at the horizon under the sun, dying to 0 by y 152 and toward x 110 and 290.
- What stays dark: the sky beyond the glow stays background texture. The grid floor below the haze stays dark, so only its lines carry it.

LINES:
- Horizon: full, solid.
- Sun outline: the arc above the horizon, half, solid.
- Floor rays: half, solid near the horizon, fading to faint toward the bottom and sides.
- Floor crossbars: half for the three nearest the horizon, faint for the rest, all solid.
- Sun reflection: one short centred dashed segment at y 147, x 180–220, faint. It is the sun's broken reflection on the floor.

ORANGE: The sun disc, stripes included, is printed in orange. It is the brightest mass and the emblem of the genre, and the stripes give the eye an immediate read that this is not just any sunset. Everything else, glow and haze included, stays in the main ink.

VARIATION:
- Jitter the sun stripes: their count (4–6), their exact y positions (±1.5) and their thickness progression.
- Shift the floor crossbars by a random phase along their perspective spacing, as if the grid were scrolling toward the viewer.
