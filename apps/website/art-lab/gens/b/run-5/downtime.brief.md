TITLE: A lighthouse at night with its lamp gone dark

IDEA: Downtime is the hour when the light that is supposed to sweep the dark stops, and the sea keeps going without it; an unlit lighthouse shows that stillness.

COMPOSITION: A flat sea horizon runs across the card at y 170, from x 90 to x 310. A small rocky base, a low irregular mound 70 wide and 10 tall, is centred at (200, 170). On it stands a tapering lighthouse tower, 44 wide at its base (y 168) and narrowing to 28 wide at its top (y 90), centred on x 200. A narrow gallery ledge 38 wide and 3 tall sits at y 88. Above it is the lamp room, a box 26 wide from y 70 to y 87, capped by a shallow dome rising to y 62 with a short finial to y 57. Inside the lamp room hangs a lamp core, a small disc of radius 3 at (200, 79). A full moon of radius 15 sits upper left at (122, 76). A loose column of reflected moonlight on the water runs below it, about 16 wide, from y 172 to y 192. The ghost of the missing beam is two straight rays from the lamp core fanning right, ending at (306, 62) and (306, 98).
