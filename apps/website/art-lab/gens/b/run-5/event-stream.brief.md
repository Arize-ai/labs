TITLE: Paper lanterns drifting down a winding river at night

IDEA: An event stream is a steady flow carrying discrete, separate things, and a river of floating lanterns shows exactly that: each light is one event, spaced in time, all borne by the same current toward the viewer.

COMPOSITION: The scene is a low perspective view of a river at night.
- **Horizon:** a horizon at y 80 spanning x 90–310.
- **River:** enters at a narrow mouth about 8 wide centred at (232, 80). It winds in a gentle S toward the viewer. ORANGE: Lantern 5 at (217, 146), mid-stream, is in orange, along with its glow and reflection. It is the single event the eye picks out of the flow, the one being inspected. Placing it partway down the river makes the eye travel the stream from mouth to foreground and land on it.

VARIATION:
- Jitter each lantern's position off the centreline by up to ±4 units, and its spacing along the river by up to ±15%, so the cadence of events feels irregular, as real streams are.
- Vary each reflection's length and the gaps in its dot breakup by a small random amount, as if the water were moving.
