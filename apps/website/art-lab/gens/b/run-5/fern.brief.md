TITLE: A single fern frond unrolling from a fiddlehead curl

IDEA: A fern grows by unrolling the same leaf pattern at every scale, so a frond still coiled at its tip shows self-similar structure being unfurled step by step.

COMPOSITION: A single frond rising diagonally from lower left to upper right.
- **Stem:** the central stem is a gentle arc from (132, 194) through (190, 140) to (236, 90).
- **Fiddlehead:** at (236, 90) the stem turns inward into a tight spiral, the fiddlehead. It is centred at (250, 98), outer radius 18, and makes about two and a half turns down to a core of radius 3.
- **Leaflets:** eleven pairs of narrow leaflets alternate along the stem, set at about 60° to it and pointing toward the tip.
  - The lowest pair, near (145, 182), is 48 long and 9 wide.
  - Each pair above is about 85% the size of the one below, down to 8 long just before the curl.
  - Each leaflet has 5–8 small rounded lobes along both edges.
  - The leaflets are slightly cupped, each tip bending upward.
- **Spiral leaflets:** inside the spiral, tiny unopened leaflets lie as short tight bumps along the coil's outer edge.
- **Base:** a short bare length of stem, 14 long, runs from (132, 194) down toward the card edge and fades out.
- **Empty field:** nothing else is on the card.

LIGHT:
- **Leaflets:** 0.6–0.75 overall. Each is shaded across its width from 0.8 on the upper-left edge to 0.45 on the lower-right edge, so every leaflet reads as a slight trough.
- **Lobes:** each lobe has a small step of light at its tip.
- **Stem:** a thin 0.5 band, brighter on its upper-left side.
- **Fiddlehead:** the brightest mass on the card. The coil is 0.9–1.0 with dots kissing.
- **Glow:** around the fiddlehead, centred on the core and about 40 in radius, falling to nothing by 0.05 at its outer edge, as if the new growth were giving off light.
- **Lower leaflets:** they dim gently toward the base, reaching 0.45 at the lowest pair, so the eye travels upward.
- **Dark areas:** the lower-right side of the card, the gaps between leaflets and the field beyond the frond stay plain background texture.

LINES:
- **Stem:** traced from base to fiddlehead core, full strength, solid.
- **Leaflet outlines:** the lobed edge of each leaflet, half strength, solid.
- **Leaflet midribs:** a short centre vein in each leaflet, faint, solid.
- **Fiddlehead coil:** its outer edge, full strength, solid.
- **Growth arc:** a dashed faint arc continuing the spiral outward by a quarter turn beyond the fiddlehead, about radius 26, hinting at the path the tip will unroll along.

ORANGE: The innermost core of the fiddlehead is orange, about the last turn of the spiral, radius 3–6, both dots and line. This is the growing point, where the next leaflet is still forming, and it is the one place on the plate that is still changing.

2. **Fiddlehead:** vary the tightness and number of turns of the curl slightly, between 2.25 and 2.75 turns.
