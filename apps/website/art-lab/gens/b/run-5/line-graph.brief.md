TITLE: A rising line graph whose newest point glows like a lit filament

IDEA: A line graph is a record of change over time, and its most recent point is where everyone looks, so the plate shows the trace climbing to a single live point at its leading edge.

COMPOSITION:
- Axes: an L with its corner at (100, 182). The vertical axis rises to (100, 62) and the horizontal axis runs to (304, 182).
- The trace: a polyline through (100, 162), (128, 150), (152, 157), (178, 128), (204, 138), (230, 106), (256, 115) and (284, 78). It is broad and readable, with three rises and two small setbacks.
- Area fill: the region between the trace and the horizontal axis, from x 100 to 284.
- Marker: a point of radius 4 centred at (284, 78), the latest reading.
- Glow around the marker: radius 26, centred at (284, 78).
- Gridlines: three horizontal gridlines at y 142, 102 and 62, spanning x 100–304.
- Data ticks: small vertical ticks, 4 units tall, on the horizontal axis below each data point's x.
- Data dots: small dots of radius 1.5 at each interior data point along the trace.

LIGHT:
- Area fill: a vertical gradient. It is about 0.55 directly under the trace and falls to 0.1 at the axis, so the dots shrink toward the baseline and the fill reads as a soft curtain hanging from the line.
- Left of each rise: add a slight extra 0.1, so light from the upper left catches the climbs.
- Marker glow: 1.0 at the core, falling off smoothly to 0 at radius 26. It is the brightest mass on the card.
- Data dots: 0.8.
- Above the trace: nothing. The background texture stays untouched.
- Outside the axes: nothing. The texture stays untouched there too.
- Upper-left quadrant: the area above the curve is left dark and empty.

LINES:
- The trace: full, solid.
- Both axes: half, solid.
- Gridlines: faint, dashed.
- Axis ticks: half, solid.
- Leader from the marker: a short faint dashed line dropping from the marker straight down to the horizontal axis at (284, 182).

ORANGE: Only the marker at (284, 78) and its glow are orange. It is "now", the leading edge of the measurement and the point the essay is about, so the eye should land there first and then read the line backward.

VARIATION:
- Jitter each interior data point's y by up to ±8, while keeping the overall upward trend and the final point the highest.
- Vary the glow radius between 22 and 30.
