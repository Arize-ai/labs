TITLE: A ray of light bending through three stacked layers of medium

IDEA: A medium is what a signal passes through and is changed by. A single ray that bends at each layer it crosses shows that the carrier shapes the message.

COMPOSITION: Three horizontal bands, each 180 wide (x 110–290) and 25 tall, stacked with no gaps:
- upper band y 95–120
- middle band y 120–145
- lower band y 145–170

A small light source, a point with a glow of radius 14, sits at (140, 58).

The ray is one continuous polyline:
- from the source (140, 58) to the entry point (185, 95) on the top surface
- through the upper band to (197, 120)
- through the middle band to (206, 145)
- through the lower band to (212, 170), so it steepens at each layer
- out the bottom, back at its original angle, to (242, 195)

A short normal stands at the entry point, x 185 from y 78 to 112. A ghost ray continues the undeviated path from (185, 95) to (228, 130).

LIGHT:
- Source: tone 1.0 at its centre, falling off over radius 14 to nothing. This is the brightest mass on the card.
- Bands: they grow denser with depth, at 0.18, 0.30 and 0.42. Each band brightens by about 0.08 along its top edge and left end, as if lit from the upper left. Each fades by 0.05 toward its lower right.
- Ray: it carries a narrow glow about 5 units wide, tone 0.5 at the ray and falling to 0. The glow is strongest above the slab and inside it.
- Background: everything above and below the slab and outside x 110–290 stays as plain texture, with no tone added.

LINES:
- Top surface of the slab (y 95) and bottom surface (y 170): full, solid, x 110–290.
- Internal interfaces at y 120 and y 145: half, solid.
- Left and right ends of the slab (x 110 and x 290, y 95–170): faint, solid.
- The ray polyline: full, solid.
- Normal at the entry point: faint, dashed.
- Ghost ray (the undeviated path): faint, dashed.

ORANGE: The ray itself (the line and its narrow glow), from source to exit. The eye should follow the signal and catch the kinks where each medium bends it. The source glow stays in main ink so that the orange begins as the beam.

VARIATION:
- Jitter each band's thickness by ±4 and its tone by ±0.05. Recompute the ray so it steepens by a slightly different, random amount in each layer, while still leaving at its original angle.
- Shift the entry point randomly along x 175–195.
