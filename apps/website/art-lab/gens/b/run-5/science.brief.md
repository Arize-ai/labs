TITLE: A convex lens gathering parallel rays to a single focus

IDEA: Science takes scattered, parallel observations and bends them through a disciplined instrument until they meet at one bright point of understanding.

COMPOSITION:
- **Optical axis:** a horizontal line at y 125, running from x 86 to x 314.
- **Lens:** a biconvex lens standing upright, centred at (170, 125). It is 124 tall (y 63–187) and 26 wide at its thickest (x 157–183). It is made of two shallow circular arcs that meet in points at top and bottom.
- **Incoming rays:** seven parallel horizontal rays enter from the left, starting at x 92. They sit at y 77, 93, 109, 125, 141, 157 and 173, and each ends at the lens face.
- **Refracted rays:** the seven rays leave the right face of the lens and converge in straight lines on a focal point at (282, 125).
- **Diverging rays:** past the focus, each ray continues a short way, diverging again to end at x 308. These stubs are about 26 long.
- **Glow:** a round glow of radius 34 centred on the focal point.

LIGHT:
- **Incoming beam:** the band between the outer rays, left of the lens (x 92–157, y 77–173), sits at an even tone of 0.18. It reads as a faint wash of light just above the background texture.
- **Lens body:** tone 0.45, shaded like polished glass.
  - It brightens to 0.75 in a crescent highlight near its upper-left edge, around (163, 90).
  - It falls to 0.25 along the lower-right rim.
- **Converging cone:** the triangle between the lens and the focus. Its tone climbs from 0.3 at the lens to 0.95 at the focus, so the dots swell as the light concentrates. This is the brightest mass on the card.
- **Focal glow:** full light at the centre, falling off smoothly to nothing at radius 34.
- **Diverging stubs:** the cone past the focus drops quickly from 0.6 to 0.1 by x 308.
- **What stays dark:** everything above y 63 and below y 187, plus the space outside the beam, stays as untouched background texture.

LINES:
- **Lens outline:** both arcs of the lens, full, solid.
- **Incoming rays:** the seven rays from x 92 to the lens, half, solid.
- **Refracted rays:** the seven rays from the lens to the focus, full, solid.
- **Diverging stubs:** the seven short rays beyond the focus, faint, solid.
- **Optical axis:** the full span from x 86 to 314, faint, dashed.

ORANGE: The focal point is the only orange element. It is a small solid dot of radius 4 at (282, 125), with the innermost core of the glow, about radius 9, also in orange. The orange wins over the white dots there. This is where every ray agrees, the moment of the result, so the eye should land on it first and then trace the rays back to the instrument.

VARIATION:
- **Ray layout:** vary the number of rays (5–9) and jitter their spacing by ±3 units.
- **Focus position:** shift the focal point by ±8 units along x, keeping the converging lines meeting exactly on it. This makes each print's lens a slightly different strength.
