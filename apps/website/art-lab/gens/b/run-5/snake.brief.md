TITLE: A sidewinder crossing a dune, tongue tasting the air

IDEA: The snake is the word itself, and drawing it mid-stride across sand, reading the air with its tongue, shows its two defining traits at once: the sinuous body and the restless sensing.

COMPOSITION:
- Body: one continuous S-curve tube. Its centreline starts at the tail tip (96, 168), rises through (140, 128), dips to a low bend at (200, 158), rises to a high bend at (252, 112) and runs level into the neck at (284, 110). The tube is 18 units thick at mid-body, tapers to 3 at the tail tip and narrows to 12 at the neck.
- Head: a blunt teardrop 30 long and 18 tall, centred at (298, 108), pointing right, its widest part at the back of the jaw.
- Eye: a dot of radius 2.5 at (304, 104).
- Tongue: a 12-unit stem from the snout at (313, 108) running out to (325, 106), then forking into two 6-unit tines that splay up and down.
- Dune: a long low ridge crest curving from (84, 190) up to (316, 172), lying just under the snake and implying the sand it crosses.
- Tracks: four short parallel J-shaped strokes, each about 20 long, slanting down and to the left. They are spaced 22 apart behind and below the body between x 110 and 190, at y 175–188.

LIGHT:
- Body: the brightest mass. The upper-left flank of each bend sits at 0.85–0.95. Across the tube the tone falls to 0.35 on the lower-right side, so the dots visibly shrink across the width and make it round. The tail fades from 0.6 to 0.2 at the tip.
- Head: 0.9 on the crown, falling to 0.4 under the jaw.
- Eye: left at 0, a dark pinhole in the bright head.
- Glow: a gentle falloff around the head and neck, 0.25 at the outline dying to nothing by 22 units out. This is the "awake" end of the animal.
- Shadow: the sand directly beneath and to the lower right of the body stays at 0, a strip about 8 units wide. It is darker than the empty background texture, which seats the snake on the ground.
- Dune: the slope below the ridge is 0.15 just under the crest and fades to 0 within 20 units. Above the ridge, leave the card alone.
- Tracks: 0.2, barely above the texture.
- Everything else is untouched background.

LINES:
- Body and head outline: full, solid. A single contour traces both edges of the tube and closes around the head and tail tip. This is the element that must read in a thumbnail.
- Belly scale line: half, dashed, running along the lower edge of the body 3 units inside the outline.
- Dune crest: faint, solid.
- Tracks: faint, dashed, one short stroke for each J.

ORANGE: The forked tongue, as a thin solid line plus its two tines. It is the smallest and most alive part of the snake and sits at the leading edge of the composition, so the eye travels along the whole S-curve to land on it.

VARIATION:
- Jitter the heights of the two bends by ±10 units and the overall phase of the S-curve slightly, so each snake moves with a different stride.
- Randomise the tongue's fork angle between 25° and 45°, and the number of tracks between 3 and 5.
