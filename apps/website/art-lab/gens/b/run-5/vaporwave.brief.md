TITLE: A sliced setting sun over an endless wireframe floor

IDEA: Vaporwave is a nostalgic, synthetic paradise, and a striped sun sinking onto an infinite neon grid is its most recognisable image: a sunset that never ends over a place that was never real.

COMPOSITION:
- **Horizon:** at y 150, spanning x 84–316.
- **Sun:** a disc of radius 58 centred at (200, 150). Only the upper half shows above the horizon, so the visible dome runs from y 92 to 150 and x 142 to 258.
- **Slices:** the lower part of the dome is cut by five horizontal gaps. Their centres sit at y 118, 127, 135, 141 and 146. The gaps thicken downward: 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 4.5 and 5.5 units tall.
- **Floor:** a perspective grid filling the area below the horizon, from y 150 to 194.
  - Eleven lines radiate from a vanishing point at (200, 150) to the bottom edge. They land evenly spaced from x 60 to x 340, every 28 units.
  - Six cross-lines get further apart as they approach the viewer, at y 152, 155, 160, 167, 178 and 194.
- **Glow:** a halo around the sun's dome, out to a radius of about 100 from (200, 150), above the horizon only.

LIGHT:
- **Sun dome:** tone 1.0 at the top, easing to about 0.85 at the horizon. It is slightly brighter toward the upper left.
- **Slice gaps:** tone 0, background texture only.
- **Halo:** tone 0.45 at the dome's rim, falling off evenly to 0 at radius 100.
- **Horizon:** a thin band of tone 0.3 just below the horizon line, between y 150 and 154 and x 130–270. It reads as light spilling onto the floor and fades sideways.
- **Floor between grid lines:** tone 0.1 near the horizon, falling to 0 by y 175.
- **Stays dark:** the upper sky above y 85, the corners, and the outer floor beyond the grid's fan.

LINES:
- **Horizon:** full strength, solid, from x 84 to 316.
- **Sun outline:** a half-strength solid arc tracing the visible dome.
- **Radiating floor lines:** half strength and solid, fading to faint at the outermost pair.
- **Floor cross-lines:** faint and solid near the horizon, rising to half strength at the nearest line, y 194.
- **Sun reflection:** one dashed faint line straight down from (200, 150) to (200, 194), sitting over the central radiating line.

ORANGE: The sun dome, including its slices and its halo, is printed in orange. It is the warm, synthetic heart of the image and the only thing that shines, so the eye lands on it before reading the cold white grid.

VARIATION:
- Jitter the slice positions by ±1.5 units and their thicknesses by ±1, so each print's stripes differ.
- Vary the sun's radius between 52 and 62, keeping its centre on the horizon.
