Mikyo has been building at AI startups since 2019 and was a founding engineer
at [Arize AI](https://arize.com). He leads open source at Arize, heading up
[Phoenix](https://github.com/Arize-ai/phoenix), the open-source platform for
tracing and evaluating agents, and co-created
[OpenInference](https://github.com/Arize-ai/openinference), the open standard
for tracing agents. With a passion for developer tools, he works at Labs on
open tooling for building agents, and on the interfaces that show people what
their agents are actually doing.
